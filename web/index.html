Connecting to Arkiv

Exchange deposit and withdrawal halts, on the record.

When an exchange shuts deposits or withdrawals for a whole network, two independent watchers write it to Arkiv. This page asks the Arkiv node directly. No Door Ledger server sits between you and the data, and a closed record cannot be edited or deleted, even by the watcher that wrote it.
